## Paula  
## AI That Turns Intentions Into Execution  
  
## The Problem  
Most productivity software assumes that humans are good planners.  
Reality says otherwise.  
People know **what** they need to do.  
They struggle with **how**, **when**, and **in what order**.  
A task like  
Finish Operating Systems Assignment  
never becomes  
* Read chapter 5 (25 min)  
* Solve Question 1 (20 min)  
* Solve Question 2 (30 min)  
* Review answers (15 min)  
* Submit before 10 PM  
Because of this, people procrastinate until deadlines become emergencies.  
They constantly underestimate effort.  
They plan unrealistically.  
They ignore difficult work.  
They miss deadlines.  
They feel guilty.  
Not because they are lazy—  
because planning itself requires executive function.  
Paula exists to perform that executive function.  
  
## Core Philosophy  
The user's only responsibility should be deciding **what** they want.  
Paula decides  
* how to do it  
* when to do it  
* how long it takes  
* where it fits  
* when it should be postponed  
* when it should be accelerated  
* when to remind again  
* when the schedule should adapt  
Planning should become automatic.  
  
## Product Vision  
Paula is an autonomous execution planner.  
Instead of maintaining static calendars or endless to-do lists,  
Paula continuously builds and rebuilds an optimal schedule based on reality.  
Schedules are living systems.  
Not fixed plans.  
  
## Core Workflow  
User enters:  
"Complete AI assignment by Friday."  
Paula automatically  
1. estimates effort  
2. breaks the work into actionable subtasks  
3. estimates duration  
4. creates a dependency graph  
5. schedules the work across available time  
6. inserts recovery breaks  
7. synchronizes with Google Calendar  
8. adjusts automatically if plans change  
The user may edit anything.  
Paula learns from those edits.  
  
## Dynamic Planning Engine  
Unlike Google Calendar,  
nothing is permanent.  
If  
* a meeting gets added  
* the user oversleeps  
* a task takes longer  
* an emergency happens  
* priorities change  
Paula automatically reschedules everything.  
The plan is always alive.

## Feature 8 — Commitment Confidence Engine  
Most productivity tools assume that if a task is placed on a calendar, it will eventually be completed.  
Reality is different.  
People consistently underestimate the time required for work, overestimate their available energy, and accept more commitments than they can realistically fulfill.  
Paula continuously estimates the probability that every important commitment will actually be completed on time.  
This confidence score is calculated using factors such as:  
* Available free time before the deadline  
* Estimated workload  
* Historical task completion rate  
* User's tendency to procrastinate  
* Similar tasks completed in the past  
* Current calendar density  
* Expected interruptions  
* Energy levels during scheduled work periods  
* Task dependencies  
Instead of simply creating a schedule, Paula tells the user whether the schedule is realistically achievable.  
Examples:  
**92% Confidence**  
"Your current plan is realistic. You should comfortably finish your AI assignment by Friday."  
**61% Confidence**  
"You may finish on time, but one unexpected delay could put you behind schedule. Consider starting one day earlier."  
**24% Confidence**  
"Based on your current workload, there is a low probability of completing this project before Friday. I recommend moving your Design Review to next week or beginning this project today."  
When confidence drops below a threshold, Paula proactively recommends changes instead of waiting for failure.  
Possible recommendations include:  
* Start the project earlier.  
* Move lower-priority tasks.  
* Extend the deadline if possible.  
* Reduce the scope of non-essential work.  
* Increase focus block duration.  
* Schedule additional deep-work sessions.  
* Delegate or collaborate where appropriate.  
The objective is not to predict failure—it is to prevent it.  
Over time, Paula learns from each completed project and continuously improves the accuracy of its predictions.  
Ultimately, the Commitment Confidence Engine transforms Paula from a task scheduler into an AI execution advisor that helps users make realistic commitments and consistently deliver on them.
